# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | **English**

## Our Pledge

In order to foster an open and friendly environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge that everyone participating in this project will be free from harassment regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Use friendly and inclusive language
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy toward other community members

## Unacceptable Behavior

- The use of sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal attacks, or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (such as their real address or email) without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Copyright and Membership Content

This project **does not provide any music resources, nor does it bypass any paid or copyright restrictions**. The following behaviors are not accepted in this project:

- Submitting code intended to crack, bypass membership authentication, or bypass regional restrictions
- Submitting features intended to mass-scrape or distribute copyrighted content
- Posting download links for copyrighted content in issues / PRs

Such content will be closed directly without further discussion.

## Scope of Responsibility

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not comply with this Code of Conduct, and to explain the reasons when appropriate. Maintainers are responsible for consistently enforcing these standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, and also applies when an individual represents this project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If there is inappropriate behavior, please contact the maintainers via issue or email. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and a necessary and appropriate response will be made. Maintainers are obligated to keep the circumstances of the reported person confidential.
